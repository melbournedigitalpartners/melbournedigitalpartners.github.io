Unicorn Sun Catchers – Window Cling Set of 6, Made in Australia

For the window the afternoon sun hits at about four o'clock, and the kid who is home by then.

The set is two unicorns, each about 7cm by 9cm, and four stars of 3 to 4cm, cut from window cling film. Cling film is why these suit a rental: there's no glue, so they hold to glass on their own, peel off cleanly, leave no residue and can be moved as often as you like. When direct sun passes through the iridescent film it scatters small rainbows across the wall, the part a photo can only half show. Designed and made in Australia.

Add the Lucky Dip sun catchers to the same order if one window isn't enough.
