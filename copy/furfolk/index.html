Penguin – Little Penguin Plush Toy, Designed in Australia

Cool attitude, warm cuddles. Ours is a little penguin, Eudyptula minor: the homegrown one.

The fur is a dense 12 to 15mm pile, which is what makes it velvety rather than fuzzy; the filling is recycled polypropylene cotton, which keeps its shape; and every toy complies with the Australian and New Zealand toy safety standard AS/NZS ISO8124.

It arrives in a brushed cotton carry bag with a free gift message option, and Australian shipping is free. The Cockatoo is the loud one, if the penguin needs company.
