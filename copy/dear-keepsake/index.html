Mia Ring – Marquise 18K Gold Plated Stainless Steel, Sizes 6–8

For hands that are in and out of water all day.

The Mia is a fine marquise ring in 18K gold plated stainless steel, designed in-house on the Gold Coast to be left on. The durability is the point: it's tarnish, water and sweat resistant, so it stays on in the shower and at the sink, and it won't go black, green or rust. It's nickel and lead free. Marquise is the pointed-oval shape, which is where the dainty look comes from.

Wear it alone or with the Tiara Ring for a mixed-metal stack. Sizes 6, 7 and 8; check the size guide first, because a ring that lives on your hand needs to fit.

Green Vanessa Ring – Stainless Steel Signet with Marquise Green CZ

For the hand that does the talking in meetings.

The Green Vanessa is our statement ring: a stainless steel signet with a marquise cut green cubic zirconia set in a bezel, designed in-house on the Gold Coast. The marquise cut, bezel-set stone is what makes it a statement rather than a plain signet, and the green is the pop of colour. The durability is the point: it's water resistant, nickel and lead free, and it won't tarnish, turn your finger green or rust.
