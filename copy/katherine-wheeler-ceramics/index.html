Flower Shaped Ceramic Paint Palette – Handmade for Watercolour

For the painter who is still mixing on a plastic tray and telling herself it's fine.

We hand-build each palette from clay in our Castlemaine studio and paint the detail by hand with ceramic underglazes, so each one is a truly original item. It's made for watercolour work, which is where a ceramic palette earns its place over plastic. At about 16cm high by 14cm wide it sits beside an A4 pad without crowding it.

Every palette is a little different because every one is painted by hand. Orders ship within one to four days by Australia Post, so it can be on the desk by next week.

  • Hand-made and hand-detailed ceramic, made in Castlemaine VIC
  • Approx. 16cm high x 14cm wide
  • Ships in 1 to 4 days via Australia Post

Porcelain Cat Ring Holder – Hand Painted Face, 10 to 13cm

For the rings that come off at the sink and end up on the windowsill.

The cat is white porcelain, formed and painted by hand in our Castlemaine studio, the face drawn on and the surface glazed. Each stands between 10 and 13cm tall on a 4.5cm base, and because each is made and painted individually, the appearance changes from one to the next: yours will be a close cousin of the cat in the photos, not a twin. It doubles as a feather holder.

The listing is for one stand; the rings shown are for display. Orders ship within one to four days by Australia Post.
