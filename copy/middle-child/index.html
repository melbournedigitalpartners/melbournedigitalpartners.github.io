Odyssey Earrings – Brass, Bayong Timber & Faux Pearl, Made in Geelong

Long, light and a little bit deco.

Odyssey hangs a bayong timber bead, a faux pearl and a hematite spacer inside a raw brass frame, so you get warmth, shine and metal in one 6cm drop. The brass is raw, not plated. At 7.5 grams each, about three or four blueberries, they hang from hypoallergenic stainless-steel studs and posts. Choose Blue, Burgundy, Olive or Amber.

Designed by Lauren Hassett, who has 20 years in product design and a weakness for art deco and mid-century, and composed by hand in our studio in the heritage-listed Federal Mills, North Geelong. Every piece is a limited edition, so the range keeps changing; the Heyday necklace is the pairing.

  • Raw brass frame, bayong timber bead, faux pearl, hematite spacer
  • Hypoallergenic stainless-steel stud and post; 6 cm long, 7.5 g each
  • Made in Geelong; Blue, Burgundy, Olive or Amber

Heyday Necklace – Enamel Flowers in Gold-Plated Brass, Made in Geelong

A small bouquet you can wear every day.

Heyday sets enamel flowers and cubic zirconia into an 18K gold-plated brass teardrop, on a 48cm hypoallergenic stainless-steel chain. The four colourways are Green/Pink, Burgundy/Blue, Orchid/Terracotta and White/Yellow, each one a different pair of flower colours against the gold.

Designed by Lauren Hassett and composed by hand in our studio in the heritage-listed Federal Mills, North Geelong, in limited-edition quantities. It pairs with the Odyssey earrings, and the limited runs are what keep the range fresh each time you look.
