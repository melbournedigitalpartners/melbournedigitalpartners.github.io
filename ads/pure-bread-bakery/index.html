Packer and Cleaner — wholesale bakery, Surrey Hills, part-time

Packing wholesale bread orders and keeping the bakery clean, working on your own without being prompted, $26.58 to $36.41 an hour. We're Pure Bread Bakery — baking in Surrey Hills since 1998 and supplying cafes across Melbourne since 2005.

About Pure Bread

We opened at 114 Union Road, Surrey Hills in 1998 with the aim of baking the best sourdough in Melbourne. We've run a wholesale operation since 2005 and now supply over 40 cafes, shops and bakeries, baking everything fresh each morning, seven days a week.

What you'll be doing

  • Packing breads for wholesale orders
  • Cleaning the bakery
  • Stocking shelves

What you'll need

  • Motivated, and able to work alone without constant prompting

What's on offer

  • $26.58 – $36.41 per hour
  • Part-time
  • Work in person at 114 Union Road, Surrey Hills

How to apply

Apply through Indeed with your availability and a short note on your work history.

Confirm before posting

  1. Days and shift times, including the start time
  2. What puts someone at the top of the $26.58–$36.41 range
  3. Any physical requirement (lifting, standing for long periods)
  4. Who applicants should email, if not through Indeed
