Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic — Dandenong South, full-time

$79,423 to $85,000 plus super on a 38-hour week, installing, servicing and repairing air conditioning and refrigeration for homes and commercial clients across Melbourne and regional Victoria. We're Noel Aircon, and we've been in the trade for over 25 years.

About Noel Aircon

We're an ARC-authorised air conditioning and refrigeration business based in Dandenong South, with over 25 years in the industry. We design, install and service split systems, cool rooms, freezer rooms and display cabinets for residential and commercial clients across metropolitan Melbourne and rural Victoria. Our clients include Westfield, Vicinity Centres, BP, Pie Face, The Groove Train and Quest Apartment Hotels.

What you'll be doing

  • Installing single-phase and three-phase air conditioning systems
  • Repairing and servicing all types of air conditioners
  • Assembling, installing, maintaining and repairing industrial, commercial and domestic air conditioning and refrigeration equipment
  • Setting out installations from drawings and specifications, including mounting brackets and pipework
  • Adjusting system controls and reassembling systems
  • Recording the cause of faults and the action taken
  • Juggling several client jobs and specifications at a time

What you'll need

  • Relevant trade qualification, or at least 3 years' relevant experience
  • Able to follow all health and safety requirements
  • Good time management and the initiative to get on with it
  • Able to work on your own and in a team, depending on the job

What's on offer

  • $79,423 – $85,000 per year plus superannuation
  • Full-time, 38 hours per week
  • Based in Dandenong South
  • Residential and commercial work across Melbourne and rural Victoria

How to apply

Email your resume to noelaircon@yahoo.com.au or call 03 9794 9105 with your qualifications and licence details.

Confirm before posting

  1. Is an ARCtick refrigerant handling licence required? State it if so
  2. Is a vehicle supplied, and are tools provided?
  3. Driver's licence requirement
  4. Start date
