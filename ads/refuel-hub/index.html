Barista (part-time) — all-day breakfast cafe, Ballarat Central, 7:45am–2pm

$26.50 to $35 an hour, part-time with regular shifts, 7:45am to 2pm — every day finishes early. We're Refuel Hub, an all-day breakfast cafe on Sturt Street in the heart of Ballarat, and we need an experienced, friendly barista.

About Refuel Hub

We're a cafe at 2 Sturt Street, Ballarat Central, serving all-day breakfast made with local produce, plus coffee and cocktails. We're open Monday to Friday 7:30am to 2pm and weekends 8am to 2pm, dine-in or takeaway.

What you'll be doing

  • Making consistently high-quality coffee and other drinks
  • Friendly, efficient service — taking orders and helping front of house
  • Keeping the coffee station clean and organised
  • Working closely with the kitchen and front-of-house team
  • Helping with opening, closing and general cafe duties as needed

What you'll need

  • Previous barista or cafe experience (1 year preferred)
  • Confident making a range of espresso-based coffees on your own during busy periods
  • Great communication and customer service
  • Reliable, punctual and a team player
  • Available across weekdays and weekends as required

What's on offer

  • $26.50 – $35 per hour
  • Part-time or casual, regular shifts, no less than 15 hours a week
  • Daytime hours: 7:45am to 2pm
  • A friendly, supportive team in central Ballarat
  • Room to grow with the business

How to apply

Apply through Indeed with your resume, a cover letter and when you could start.

Confirm before posting

  1. Days per week, and how weekdays and weekends are split
  2. What earns the top of the $26.50–$35 range
  3. The coffee you serve (roaster) and the machine — baristas ask
  4. Team size
