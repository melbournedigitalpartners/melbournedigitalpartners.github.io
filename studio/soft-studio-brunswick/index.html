How it works

Three rules, run from your check-in report.

1
Book from the normal timetable

Any Soft Studio class at your level counts: Barre, Barre Balletic, Barre & Stretch, Pilates Flow, Pilates Strength, Move, Stretch, Breathe. Every class on your timetable is open level, so every class counts.

2
Hit the weekly target

Two classes a week for the first fortnight, then three. Check in at the desk or in the app as usual; the check-in is the tally.

3
Watch the board

One point per class, from your check-ins. A tick for every week you hit target. Posted Sunday night from the check-in report.

Weekly targets

Dates are Monday to Sunday. The mid-point check-in and the finish-week class are on your timetable, not extra sessions.
