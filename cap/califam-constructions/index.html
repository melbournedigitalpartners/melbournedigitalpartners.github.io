Califam Constructions

Family-owned Melbourne sewer and hydraulic infrastructure contractor established 1974.

Who we are

Califam Constructions is a family-owned civil contractor founded in 1974 by Michael Caligiuri and now run with his sons. It constructs gravity sewer reticulation and trunk outfalls, rising mains, pump stations, drainage and water supply works across greater Melbourne, including live sewer, deep excavation and trenchless work. It is an accredited sewer contractor with four Melbourne water authorities and certified to ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001.
