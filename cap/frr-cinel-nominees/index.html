Who we are

FRR Cinel Nominees is a Laverton North contractor with more than 40 years in Victoria. It provides civil engineering and road construction, sewer, water and drainage reticulation, power and telecommunications works: transmission and distribution, VEDN and commercial and domestic building. Its clients are private developers, local government and electricity distribution companies, and it holds VicRoads R1 prequalification and multiple distributor accreditations.
