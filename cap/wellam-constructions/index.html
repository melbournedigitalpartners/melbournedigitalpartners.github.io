Who we are

Wellam Constructions is a locally owned and managed earthmoving and civil construction business in South Geelong, formed in 1940. It builds subdivisional estates and residential, industrial and commercial developments, covering bulk earthworks, sewer and stormwater reticulation, pavements, streetscaping and service installation. It is ISO 9001/14001/45001 certified and prequalified with VicRoads, Barwon Water and VEDN.
