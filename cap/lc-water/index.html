LC Water

Geelong process engineering firm designing and building water and wastewater treatment plants.

Who we are

LC Water is a process engineering house founded in Geelong in 2001 by Laurie Curran and now directed by Laurie Curran and Jarrod Fraim. It designs, constructs and commissions bespoke water and wastewater treatment plants, including DAF/DAFF, membrane, reverse osmosis, chemical dosing and activated sludge systems, for water authorities and industrial users. It operates across Australia and the subcontinent with offices in Geelong and Karachi.
