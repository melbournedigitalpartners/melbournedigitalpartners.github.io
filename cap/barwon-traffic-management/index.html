Geelong-based 24/7 traffic management provider operating across Victoria since 2010.

Who we are

Barwon Traffic Management is a South Geelong traffic management company established in 2010. It prepares traffic management plans and guidance schemes, handles permit applications and provides 24/7 traffic control, temporary signals, TMA vehicles and VMS board hire. Clients include government agencies, councils, civil contractors, utilities and event organisers.
