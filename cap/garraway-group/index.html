Mildura-based earthmoving, civil and mining services contractor operating Australia-wide.

Who we are

Garraway Group is an earthmoving, civil construction and mining services business based in Mildura that started in the Sunraysia district over 90 years ago and now works across Australia. It serves the agriculture, resources and construction industries with mine rehabilitation, tailings and water storage construction, bulk earthworks, road construction and plant hire, using an extensive mostly Caterpillar fleet with GPS and laser guidance.
