D.A. Beretta Plumbing & Gasfitting

Geelong family-owned commercial and industrial plumbing, gasfitting and civil services contractor.

Who we are

D.A. Beretta is a locally owned family plumbing business based in Moolap, Geelong, delivering commercial and industrial plumbing and gasfitting across Victoria for around 60 years. Its work covers water main replacement, sewer, directional boring, hydro excavation, CCTV, leak detection, fire services and mechanical services for water authorities, road and rail, government and civil clients. It is certified under the CCF Management Code and is a Master Plumbers and CCF member.
