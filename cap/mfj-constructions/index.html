Who we are

MFJ Constructions is a Preston-based infrastructure contractor with more than 40 years serving water authorities and developers across Greater Melbourne. It builds branch sewers, reticulation, pumping stations, water distribution mains, PRVs and treatment plant works, including trenchless construction. Clients include Yarra Valley Water, Melbourne Water, South East Water, City West Water and major head contractors.
