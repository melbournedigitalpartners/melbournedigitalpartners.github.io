Who we are

Newearth Constructions is a family-owned civil engineering and earthmoving contractor established in 1927 and based in Romsey, Central Victoria. It delivers land development, roadworks, drainage, dams and bulk earthworks for local and state government and developers across Central Victoria and Melbourne's outer north, and holds DTP R3/F25 prequalification plus approvals with several water authorities.
