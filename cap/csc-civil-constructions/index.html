CSC Civil Constructions

Vermont-based civil contractor for subdivisions, retirement villages, car parks and road works across Melbourne.

Who we are

CSC Civil Constructions was founded in 1990 by Ron Scoble and is based in Vermont, operating across metropolitan Melbourne. It built its early niche in greenfield subdivisions, bulk earthworks and road construction in outer south-east Melbourne. Today it focuses on retirement villages, medium-density developments, subdivisions, car parks, drainage and pavement works, with VicRoads R2 and Places Victoria C2M accreditation.
