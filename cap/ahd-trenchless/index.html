Who we are

AHD Trenchless, established in 2009, designs and installs pipelines by horizontal directional drilling for the water, energy and mining industries, specialising in large diameter gravity sewer and river crossings for major Australian utilities. It installs pipe from 63 mm to 1200 mm over 100 m to 1.5 km and describes its rig range as Australia's largest and most advanced, including patented technology.
