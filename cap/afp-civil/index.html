Who we are

AFP Civil is a family-owned civil construction and earthmoving company based at Warrenheip near Ballarat. It started in 1996 as Andrew Peterkin Truck & Backhoe Hire and later rebranded as its services expanded. It delivers subdivisions, roads, drainage, pipelines and earthworks for government, utility, developer and private clients, and is a preferred contractor for Central Highlands Water.
