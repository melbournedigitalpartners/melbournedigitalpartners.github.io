Who we are

Jarvis Delahey Contractors (JDC) is a locally owned civil contractor based in Shepparton, co-founded in December 2004 by Richard Jarvis and Nathan Delahey. It delivers subdivisions, carparks, drainage, streetscapes and site works across the Goulburn Valley, largely for local councils, using Leica 3D GPS machine guidance. The company is VicRoads pre-qualified, VEDN accredited and ISO certified.
