NVC Precast

Kilmore manufacturer of prestressed and precast concrete bridge components since 1989.

Who we are

NVC Precast is the trading name of North-Vic Constructions Pty Ltd, founded in 1989 by Dan Kleinitz and based in Kilmore on the Hume corridor. It designs and manufactures prestressed and precast concrete products for civil infrastructure, particularly bridge beams, crossheads, piles, deck slabs and abutments, supplying Victoria and southern New South Wales. It holds ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 certification and VicRoads and RMS prequalification.
