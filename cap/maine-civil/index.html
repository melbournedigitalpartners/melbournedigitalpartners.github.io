Who we are

Maine Civil is a locally owned civil construction contractor based in East Bendigo, established in 2015 and led by two directors with a combined 60+ years of civil experience. It delivers civil infrastructure projects for developers and local authorities across Central Victoria, and offers wet and dry plant hire, heavy haulage and water cartage. The company employs 40+ staff and runs 35+ major plant items and trucks.
