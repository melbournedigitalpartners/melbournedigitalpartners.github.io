Southwest Traffic Control

Timboon-based traffic management provider for construction, roadworks and events in south-west Victoria.

Who we are

Southwest Traffic Control is a safety-focused traffic management company based in Timboon in south-west Victoria. It provides traffic control, traffic management plans, permits and authorisations for road reserve worksites, construction sites and public events. The company is VicRoads pre-qualified for TGS and TMP work and a member of the Traffic Management Association of Australia.
