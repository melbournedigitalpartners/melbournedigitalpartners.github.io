Central Vic Civil Pty Ltd

Ballarat civil contractor for pipelines, earthworks, roads, water infrastructure and heavy haulage.

Who we are

Central Vic Civil is a Ballarat-based civil construction company citing more than 30 years of experience. It builds small and large diameter pipelines, sewer and water pump stations, treatment plant infrastructure, roads, subdivisions and bulk earthworks for government authorities and private developers across Central Victoria, and runs a transport and heavy haulage division. It is certified to ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001.
