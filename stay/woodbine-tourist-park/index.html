3 Days Around Lakes Entrance, from Woodbine Tourist Park

9 stops, morning to evening, all within a day trip of the park. Book the nights, then follow the days.

Day 1A mixed day

1 · Morning
Raymond Island koalas, by ferry from Paynesville (opens in a new tab)

A free ferry crosses the McMillan Strait from Paynesville to Raymond Island, one of the best koala-spotting places in Victoria, with more than 60 bird species.

Drive time: added when you confirm
2 · Afternoon
Nyerimilang Heritage Park (opens in a new tab)

A heritage park on the shores of the Gippsland Lakes, named on the park’s own list of local attractions.

Drive time: added when you confirm
3 · Late in the day
Metung village and the midday pelicans (opens in a new tab)

A small lakeside village west of Lakes Entrance with waterfront cafes and restaurants; pelicans are fed outside the Metung pub at midday each day.

Drive time: added when you confirm

Day 2A mixed day

4 · Morning
Gippsland Lakes cruise (opens in a new tab)

Boat cruises and eco tours run from town onto the Gippsland Lakes.

Drive time: added when you confirm
5 · Afternoon
Ninety Mile Beach via the footbridge (opens in a new tab)

The footbridge over the Cunninghame Arm leads from town to Ninety Mile Beach, a surf beach patrolled in the summer months.

Drive time: added when you confirm
6 · Late in the day
Fresh seafood on the Esplanade (opens in a new tab)

Lakes Entrance is a fishing-fleet town; the fresh seafood shops around town sell the catch.

Drive time: added when you confirm

Day 3A mixed day

7 · MorningTickets
Buchan Caves Reserve (opens in a new tab)

Limestone caves carved by an underground river almost 400 million years ago. Guided tours of the Fairy Cave and Royal Cave; tickets must be bought 24 hours ahead.

Check Parks Victoria for closures before you go (opens in a new tab)

Drive time: added when you confirm
8 · Afternoon
Lake Tyers State Park (opens in a new tab)

7,100 hectares of coast, rainforest gullies, eucalypt forest and wetlands for walking, fishing and picnics. Access roads are unsealed.

Marsdenia Rainforest Walk is closed for bridge and boardwalk repairs. Check Parks Victoria before you go (opens in a new tab)

From the park · Parks Victoria: about 20 km from Lakes Entrance.
