Who we are

Oak Park Community Sports Association in one page, for a business that has not met us yet.

Oak Park Football Club was established in 1957 when the under 15 team was fielded in the local junior league, joined the EDFL in 1960 and has won over twenty premierships across all levels, including consecutive A Grade flags in 2002 and 2003. Combining Football, Cricket, Darts, Netball and Anglers, we offer different levels of sports and divisions to cater to all.

Why local sponsorship works

Public data, cited below, not club estimates.

85.4%

of Victorian adults, about 4.8 million people, took part in community sport or active recreation at least once in 2023–24; 67.4% did so weekly.1

750,000

Victorians volunteered in community sport and active recreation in 2023–24.1

$1,161

is the average entry-level cost of playing sport for a child in Australia, counting registration, uniform and equipment; $1,200 for an adult.2

$40.3m

was raised for community sport through the Australian Sports Foundation in FY25, from more than 30,000 donations.3

The Australian Sports Foundation puts it plainly: “some of the best sponsorships come from local businesses who value community involvement.”4

  1. Parliament of Victoria, Parliamentary Library & Information Service, Community sport and active recreation in Victoria: changes and challenges (June 2025), pp. 2, 6 and 12.
  2. Australian Sports Commission, Cost of Sport study (Federation University), as reported by Australasian Leisure Management, 31 August 2026.
  3. Australian Sports Foundation, Fundraising for Sport Reaches Record $118.8 Million in FY25, 10 October 2025.
  4. Australian Sports Foundation, How to Get Sponsorships for a Sports Team, 17 June 2025.
